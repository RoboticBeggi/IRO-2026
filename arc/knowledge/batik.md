Batik is cloth decorated by drawing with hot wax, so the waxed parts resist the dye.
Malaysian batik is often hand-drawn with a small pen called a canting, or printed with a metal block.
It is known for bright colours and nature motifs such as flowers and leaves, usually without human or animal figures.
