The tepak sirih is a decorated box that holds everything for chewing sirih: betel leaves, slices of areca nut (pinang), lime paste (kapur) and gambier.
Offering sirih to a guest is a Malay sign of welcome and respect.
It has an important place in Malay weddings and engagement ceremonies, where the tepak sirih is carried and presented first.
It also appears in welcoming ceremonies for honoured guests.
