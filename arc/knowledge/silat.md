Silat is the traditional Malay martial art of self-defence. It is also an art: its flowing movements are often performed to music of drums and the serunai.
Before a performance, the pesilat greets the audience and the teacher with graceful opening movements.
Silat teaches discipline, respect and self-control, not only fighting.
In 2019, UNESCO inscribed Silat from Malaysia on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
