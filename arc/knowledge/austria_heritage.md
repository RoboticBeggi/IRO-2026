The Spanish Riding School in Vienna keeps the art of classical horsemanship alive with its white Lipizzaner horses; this tradition is on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
The Viennese coffee house culture, where people sit for hours with a coffee, a glass of water and newspapers, is part of Austria's national inventory of intangible cultural heritage.
Like wayang kulit in Malaysia, these traditions live only as long as people keep practising them and passing them on.
