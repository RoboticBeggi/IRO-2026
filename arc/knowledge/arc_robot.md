ARC stands for Autonomous Reasoning Companion. It was built by a team of students from Malaysia for the International Robot Olympiad 2026 in Vienna.
Its brain is a small computer, an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, and everything runs offline: no internet is needed.
A microphone array hears the visitor and finds which direction the voice comes from.
A robot arm with six motors and a camera on its wrist can look closely at heritage objects, pick them up and perform movements.
A depth camera measures objects and sees people, and a laser scanner (LiDAR) senses who is approaching.
It recognises heritage objects after seeing them only briefly, and it speaks Bahasa Melayu, English and German.
