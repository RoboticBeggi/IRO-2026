---
title: Songket (gold-thread woven cloth)
names: [songket, kain songket, woven cloth, Brokat]
verified: false

---
Songket is a hand-woven cloth in which gold or silver threads are woven between the silk or cotton threads, so shining patterns float on the fabric.
It is woven on a wooden loom, thread by thread; one piece can take weeks or months.
Terengganu and Kelantan are famous for songket. It is worn at weddings and royal ceremonies.
In 2021, UNESCO inscribed Malaysian songket on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
