Mak Yong is a traditional dance theatre from Kelantan that combines acting, singing, dance, music and improvised comedy.
The main hero, Pak Yong, is traditionally played by a woman.
UNESCO proclaimed Mak Yong a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2005, and it is now on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
It was banned in Kelantan for about twenty years; the ban was lifted in 2019 with conditions.
